\# NF-004 Retention and backup restore operations



\## Where the procedures are documented

\- docs/OPERATIONS.md, sections "Backups and restore" and "Retention and archival"

\- scripts/backup.sh: copies SQLite (local mode) or runs mysqldump

&#x20; (--single-transaction, --routines, --triggers) for MySQL

\- scripts/restore.sh: restores SQLite or MySQL backups



\## Retention policy

\- RETENTION\_YEARS = 10, with a matching retention\_years SystemConfig

\- No automatic destructive cleanup task exists

\- Archival of academic records, documents and audit logs is a reviewed,

&#x20; manual process to encrypted durable storage (per OPERATIONS.md)
