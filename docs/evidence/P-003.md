# P-003 Sprint 1 Working-Product Demo

## One-minute run of show

Use a running local or review deployment with synthetic/demo data. Keep
credentials, secrets, and personal data out of the recording.

| Time | Demonstration |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Identify the Student Record System and state the actual environment and commit being demonstrated. |
| 0:08–0:23 | Show the sign-in page and sign in with a prepared demo account; do not reveal its password. |
| 0:23–0:42 | Show the authenticated portal dashboard and point out the role-specific navigation. |
| 0:42–0:55 | Open one available portal section and briefly show the working page without making unverified behavior claims. |
| 0:55–1:00 | Close by stating what the clip demonstrated and any material limitation. |

Keep the clip to approximately one minute. Prefer a single continuous capture so
the transition from sign-in to the authenticated product is visible.
